Privacy Policy for Nightfall Ninja

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Nightfall Ninja ("the app") is developed by Shashwat Samadhiya. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play the app.

Summary

Nightfall Ninja does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, does not connect to the internet, and contains no advertising or analytics software.

What the app stores

The app saves a small amount of gameplay data — such as your high score and sound on/off preference — directly on your device using standard local device storage. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted anywhere, and is not accessible to the developer or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or any other sensitive device permission.

Third parties

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and does not share any information with third parties, because it does not collect any information to share.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future update adds online features, ads, or analytics — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

Contact
